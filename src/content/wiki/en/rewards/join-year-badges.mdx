The Hunt: Roblox 20's hub doubled as a **veteran check**: it read your account's creation year and awarded a matching “I Joined In” badge — one for every year from 2006 to 2026. The result is a fascinating census of the event's player base. All counts from the official badge API, September 29, 2026.

## What the numbers say

- **I Joined In 2006!?!?** is the rarest badge of the entire event: just **173** first-year veterans showed up. The escalating punctuation (!?!?) is doing a lot of work, and deservedly.
- Counts climb every single year — the newest players (2026 joiners, 5,540,141) outnumber 2006 veterans roughly 32,000 to 1.
- Twenty-three matching catalog accessories (**The Hunt: Roblox 20 Badge (2004)** through **(2026)**) cover even the pre-launch Dynablocks years of 2004–2005.

If you joined the hub during the event, your year badge is already in your inventory — it was one of the few things in the event you could not miss.
