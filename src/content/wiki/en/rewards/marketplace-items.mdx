Eleven Robux items were sold during the event (September 17–28, 2026). Prices from 105 Robux to 2,000,000.

## All 11

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Confetti Cannon** | 2,000 Robux |
| **Birthday Time Fedora** | 50,000 Robux |
| **Vicennial Party Hat** | 105 Robux |
| **Candles of Pwnage** | 13,337 Robux |
| **Vicennial Balloons** | 145 Robux |
| **Agonizingly Frosted Birthday Bucket of Doom** | 100,000 Robux |
| **The Rest of the Cake** | 500 Robux |
| **Crown-fetti Cake** | 4,000 Robux |

| **Shedletsekkar** | 2,000,000 Robux ([[limited unique]]) |

## Notes

- **Shedletsekkar — 2,000,000 Robux, limited unique.** The whale item. Since it's limited, it's the one event item that can actually change hands after the event.
- **Candles of Pwnage — 13,337 Robux.** The price is the joke: a nod to the classic Horns of Pwnage.
- **Vicennial Party Hat (105)** and **Vicennial Balloons (145)** were the cheap souvenirs. “Vicennial” = 20th anniversary.

The free side of the event is on [all rewards](/rewards/all-rewards/).
