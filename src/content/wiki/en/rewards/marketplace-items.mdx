Alongside the free quest rewards, The Hunt: Roblox 20 sold **11 premium avatar items** through the marketplace during the event window (September 17–28, 2026). Prices ran from 105 Robux to a headline **2,000,000 Robux**.

## All 11 marketplace items

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Confetti Cannon** | 2,000 Robux |
| **Birthday Time Fedora** | 50,000 Robux |
| **Vicennial Party Hat** | 105 Robux |
| **Candles of Pwnage** | 13,337 Robux |
| **Vicennial Balloons** | 145 Robux |
| **Agonizingly Frosted Birthday Bucket of Doom** | 100,000 Robux |
| **The Rest of the Cake** | 500 Robux |
| **Crown-fetti Cake** | 4,000 Robux |
| **Opal King of the Night** | 50,000 Robux |

| **Telamon’s Other Crafting Jewel** | 1,000 Robux |

## The ones worth remembering

- **Shedletsekkar — 2,000,000 Robux, limited unique.** The event's whale item and one of the most expensive Robux items ever sold in an event. Being limited unique, it is the one Hunt: Roblox 20 item with a genuine after-market.
- **Agonizingly Frosted Birthday Bucket of Doom — 100,000 Robux** and **Birthday Time Fedora — 50,000 Robux**, the other five-figure pieces.
- **Candles of Pwnage — 13,337 Robux.** The price is the tell: a nod to the classic Horns of Pwnage series.
- **Vicennial Party Hat (105 Robux)** and **Vicennial Balloons (145 Robux)** were the entry-level souvenirs — “vicennial” = twentieth anniversary.

The event also revived dormant accessory series after a decade away — Dominus, Sparkle Time Fedora, Crowns of the Night, Teapot, Builders Club Hard Hat, Empyrean Reignment, Paintball Mask, Bighead and Visor pieces all appear across the free and paid item pools (see [all rewards](/rewards/all-rewards/) for the free side).
