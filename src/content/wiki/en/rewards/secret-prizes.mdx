## Why so rare?

Nothing in-game told you these quests existed, and some had absurd triggers — a phone number punched into a spawn-location keypad, a hidden control-room switch. Four of them, including the rarest (**How To Build A Happy Home**), still had no documented solution when the event ended.

Getting all 20 was required for the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/). Compare with the [main prizes](/rewards/main-prizes/) to see the drop-off.
