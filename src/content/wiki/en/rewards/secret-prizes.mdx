Beyond the 20 main quests, every participating game in The Hunt: Roblox 20 hid a **secret quest** — unmarked, unprompted, and worth a separate avatar item. These 20 secret prizes are the event's true completionist set. This page ranks all of them by rarity (official badge API, September 29, 2026); step-by-step walkthroughs are in the [secret quests guide](/guides/secret-quests/).

## Why so rare?

Three factors kept secret counts low: nothing in the games told you a second quest existed, several required absurdly specific trigger sequences (a phone number punched into a spawn-location keypad in Rocket Arena; a hidden control-room variable flip in Natural Disaster Survival), and four of them — including the rarest, **How To Build A Happy Home** — were still marked “TBA” on the community wiki when the event ended.

Clearing all 20 secrets was not just for completionists: it was **required** to unlock the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) and its Amictus Infinitum reward. Compare against the [main prizes](/rewards/main-prizes/) to see the drop-off.
