The Hunt: Roblox 20 had two grand prizes: the **Amictus Infinitum** and the **Dominus Infinitum**. You earned the Amictus by finishing everything. Roblox never explained how the Dominus would be given out.

Looking for who actually won? That's on the [winners page](/guides/winners/), with every badge check we've done.

## Amictus Infinitum

The completion prize: clear all 20 main quests and all 20 [secret quests](/guides/secret-quests/), then solve the noob sliding puzzle that unlocked in the hub on September 27. **153,015 players** got it — 0.5% of the 30M hub visitors. Solving the puzzle also teleported you to an ending room with NPC versions of Builderman, erik.cassel, Telamon and Matt Dusek. Walkthrough: [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/).

## Dominus Infinitum

The headline item. It's the first event Dominus since Ready Player One in 2018, and only the second event ever to offer one.

Roblox didn't publish a way to earn it. It also hasn't said who gets it or when. The event leaderboard is the likely source, but that's a guess, not a confirmation.

For the current status (still nobody, as of October 15, 2026), see [The Hunt: Roblox 20 winners](/guides/winners/).
