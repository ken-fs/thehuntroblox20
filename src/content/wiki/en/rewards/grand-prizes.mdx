Two items topped The Hunt: Roblox 20. One went out to over a hundred thousand players; the other went to nobody — at least not yet.

## Amictus Infinitum

The completion prize: clear all 20 main quests and all 20 [secret quests](/guides/secret-quests/), then solve the noob sliding puzzle that unlocked in the hub on September 27. **143,591 players** got it (7.1% completion). Solving the puzzle also teleported you to an ending room with NPC versions of Builderman, erik.cassel, Telamon and Matt Dusek. Walkthrough: [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/).

## Dominus Infinitum

The headline item — the first event Dominus since Ready Player One in 2018 — and as of September 29, 2026 its badge shows **zero awards**. Roblox hasn't said who gets it or when. The event leaderboard is the likely source, but that's inference, not confirmation. We watch the badge counter and update the [winners page](/guides/winners/) the moment it moves.

*Counts: Roblox badge API, September 29, 2026.*
