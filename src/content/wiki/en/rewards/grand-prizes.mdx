Two items sat at the top of The Hunt: Roblox 20: the **Amictus Infinitum** and the **Dominus Infinitum** — the first Dominus awarded in a Roblox event since Ready Player One in 2018. They played very different roles.

## Amictus Infinitum — the earned crown

The **Amictus Infinitum** was the completionist grand prize: finish all 20 main quests and all 20 secret quests, then solve the noob-themed sliding puzzle that unlocked in the hub on September 27 at 11 AM EST. **143,591 players** earned it (a 7.1% completion rate) — a small club, but an open one. Solving the puzzle also teleported players to an ending area with NPC dialog from Builderman, erik.cassel, Telamon and Matt Dusek. Full walkthrough: [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/).

## Dominus Infinitum — the unwon crown

The **Dominus Infinitum** is the event's headline item — and as of September 29, 2026, its official badge shows **zero awards**. Roblox has not yet published how or to whom it will be distributed; the event's structure (a leaderboard feeding the final puzzle) points to top finishers. We are tracking the badge API and the [winners page](/guides/winners/) — the moment the first Dominus lands in an inventory, it shows up there.

## Why it matters

Event Dominuses are generational items: the last one came from Ready Player One (2018), and the Dominus series itself had been dormant for over a decade before this event. Whatever the final award count ends up being, the Dominus Infinitum will be one of the rarest modern accessories on the platform.
