The 20 main quests were the core of The Hunt: Roblox 20 — one in each game, each paying out a year-themed item made by a community UGC creator. All closed now. Counts: Roblox badge API, September 29, 2026.

## Notes

- **Most earned:** Crossroads Tower, 7,214,915. It was the first island most players cleared.
- **Least earned:** The Last Deflection, 1,956,388.
- **Fun fact:** the official badge for the 2007 item is spelled “Dual of the Heights” in the API — a typo. The catalog item is **Duel of the Heights**.
- Clearing all 20 main quests plus all 20 [secret quests](/rewards/secret-prizes/) unlocked the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/).
