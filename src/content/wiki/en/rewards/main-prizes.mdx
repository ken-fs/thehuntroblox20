The backbone of The Hunt: Roblox 20 was its **20 main quests** — one inside each participating game, each awarding a year-themed avatar item designed by community UGC creators. This page lists every main prize, its exact objective, and how many players earned it. Data: official Roblox badge API, September 29, 2026.

## Highlights

- **Most earned:** the Crossroads Tower (7,214,915 awards) — Crossroads was the first island most players cleared.
- **Rarest main prize:** The Last Deflection (1,956,388) — Blade Ball's ticket-collecting quest filtered out the most players.
- **A naming quirk:** the official badge for the 2007 prize is literally named “Dual of the Heights” in the Roblox API — a typo for the catalog item **Duel of the Heights**. We keep the item's correct spelling everywhere except direct badge quotes.
- **UGC creators:** every main prize was designed by a community creator (Reverse_Polarity, Spectaqual, FileSaved, inkwaves, wuvenote, MiracleDrops and more) — the credits live on each [game page](/games/).

## Beyond the main line

Finishing a main quest also fed two bigger goals: collecting all 20 main prizes (plus all 20 [secret prizes](/rewards/secret-prizes/)) was the entry requirement for the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) and the **Amictus Infinitum**, and each quest pushed you up the event leaderboard that decided the [grand prize winners](/guides/winners/).
