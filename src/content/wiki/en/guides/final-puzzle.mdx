The final puzzle was the endgame of The Hunt: Roblox 20 — a locked trophy in the middle of the hub that opened on **September 27, 2026 at 11 AM EST**, a day before the event ended.

## Getting in

You needed **all 20 main quests and all 20 secret quests** done. Top leaderboard players got in a few minutes early.

## The puzzle

A **noob-themed sliding puzzle**. If you ever need to solve one like it: build row by row from the top, never disturb finished rows, and solve the last two rows together by cycling tiles. Creator footage of a full clear is embedded below.

## The ending

Clearing it gave the **Amictus Infinitum** and teleported you to an ending room: display givers for both grand prizes (the Dominus one was decorative — still [zero awards](/guides/winners/)) and NPC dialog from Builderman, erik.cassel, Telamon and Matt Dusek.

## Numbers

**143,591 players** solved it — 7.1% of those who tried, out of 29,902,983 hub visitors. More: [grand prizes](/rewards/grand-prizes/) · [secret quests guide](/guides/secret-quests/).
