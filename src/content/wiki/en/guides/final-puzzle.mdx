The final puzzle was The Hunt: Roblox 20's endgame — a trophy that stood locked in the center of the event hub until **September 27, 2026 at 11 AM EST**, one day before the event closed. Solving it awarded the **Amictus Infinitum** and a ticket to the event's hidden ending.

## Entry requirements

The puzzle only opened to players who had completed **all 20 main quests and all 20 secret quests** — the full 40. Players at the top of the event leaderboard were let in a few minutes before the public unlock.

## The puzzle itself

A **noob-themed sliding puzzle** — a fitting final exam for a 20th-anniversary event. Sliding-puzzle solutions are pattern-based: solve row by row from the top, locking completed rows and never disturbing them, and finish the last two rows together by cycling tiles. Creator footage of a completed run is embedded below for anyone reconstructing the exact board.

## The ending

Clearing the puzzle granted the Amictus Infinitum and teleported players to a dedicated **ending area**: display givers for the Amictus Infinitum and Dominus Infinitum (the Dominus giver was decorative — its badge still shows [zero awards](/guides/winners/)), and dialog with NPC versions of Roblox founders and elders — **Builderman, erik.cassel, Telamon and Matt Dusek** — a quiet hall-of-fame moment to close the anniversary.

## The numbers

**143,591 players** solved the puzzle before the event ended on September 28 — 7.1% of those who attempted it, out of 29,902,983 hub visitors. For the item itself, see [grand prizes](/rewards/grand-prizes/); for the 40 quests that gated it, the [secret quests guide](/guides/secret-quests/).
