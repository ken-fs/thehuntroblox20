**Status: final — last updated September 29, 2026.** The Hunt: Roblox 20 ended September 28 at 10 AM PT. Here is everything publicly verifiable about who won what, sourced from the official Roblox badge API.

## What we know

- **Amictus Infinitum: 143,591 winners.** Everyone who completed all 40 quests and solved the [final sliding puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) received it before the event closed. This is the complete and final list of “winners” in the classic sense.
- **Dominus Infinitum: 0 awarded.** The badge exists in the official API but had never been granted when we captured the data on September 29. Roblox has not published a winner list, a count, or a distribution method.
- **The likely mechanism:** the event ran a leaderboard, and top leaderboard players accessed the final puzzle a few minutes before everyone else on September 27 — the same leaderboard is the expected source of Dominus recipients. This is inference, not confirmation; treat any name list circulating without an official source as unverified.

## How we track it

This page watches the Dominus Infinitum badge counter. The moment it moves above zero, the winners exist and we update this page — check back, or watch the badge's award count yourself on the official event place.
