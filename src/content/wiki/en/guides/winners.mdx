**Nobody has won the Dominus Infinitum yet.** On October 15, 2026, its badge still shows **0 awards**. The event ended September 28, so that's 17 days with no winner.

The other grand prize, the Amictus Infinitum, went to **153,015 players**. Those are the only confirmed winners of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Dominus Infinitum winners: the badge counter so far

We read both grand-prize badges straight from Roblox's badge API. Here's every check we've done.

| Date checked | Dominus Infinitum awards | Amictus Infinitum awards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sep 29, 2026 (day after the event) | 0 | 143,591 |
| Oct 11, 2026 | 0 | 153,015 |
| Oct 15, 2026 | 0 | 153,015 |

The takeaway: the Amictus counter did move after the event, so the API isn't stuck. The Dominus zero is real.

## What we know

- **Amictus Infinitum: 153,015 winners.** Everyone who cleared all 40 quests and the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) got it. Late grants added 9,424 between September 29 and October 11. Since then the count hasn't moved, and the API shows 0 new awards in the past day. Treat 153,015 as the final number.
- **Dominus Infinitum: still 0 awarded.** The badge exists and is enabled, but it has never been granted. Roblox hasn't said who gets it or when.
- **The hub hasn't said anything either.** Roblox updated the hub on October 8, 2026. Its description still only says the event ran September 17–28. There's no mention of the Dominus.
- **The leaderboard is the likely source.** Top leaderboard players got into the final puzzle a few minutes early on September 27. That makes them the obvious Dominus pick, but it's a guess, not a confirmation.

## Fake winner lists

If you see a "Dominus Infinitum winners" list on social media, be careful. The badge says nobody has it. Any list without an official Roblox source is a rumor for now.
