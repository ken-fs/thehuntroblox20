**Status: October 11, 2026.** The event ended September 28. Here's what's actually verifiable, twelve days on.

## What we know

- **Amictus Infinitum: 153,015 winners.** Everyone who cleared all 40 quests and the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) got it — and the counter keeps climbing, up 9,424 since the September 29 snapshot. Late completions are still being processed.
- **Dominus Infinitum: still 0 awarded.** Twelve days after the event closed, the badge exists in the API and has never been granted. Roblox hasn't announced who gets it or when — and the longer the silence runs, the more likely the grant is a deliberate future moment rather than a pending batch.
- Top leaderboard players got into the final puzzle a few minutes early on September 27 — so the leaderboard is the likely source of Dominus recipients. Inference, not confirmation. Treat any “winner list” without an official source as rumor.

## How we track it

We watch the Dominus Infinitum badge counter. The day it moves off zero, this page updates. For reference, the Amictus counter moved from 143,591 (Sep 29) to 153,015 (Oct 11) — proof the counters are live, which is what makes the Dominus zero meaningful rather than a stale API read.

Everything else's final counts are on [all rewards](/rewards/all-rewards/) and the [event recap](/guides/event-recap/).
