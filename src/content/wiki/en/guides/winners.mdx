**Status: September 29, 2026.** The event ended September 28. Here's what's actually verifiable.

## What we know

- **Amictus Infinitum: 143,591 winners.** Everyone who cleared all 40 quests and the [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) got it. That's the complete list.
- **Dominus Infinitum: 0 awarded.** The badge exists in the API; it's never been granted. Roblox hasn't announced who gets it or when.
- Top leaderboard players got into the final puzzle a few minutes early on September 27 — so the leaderboard is the likely source of Dominus recipients. Inference, not confirmation. Treat any “winner list” without an official source as rumor.

## How we track it

We watch the Dominus Infinitum badge counter. The day it moves off zero, this page updates.

Everything else's final counts are on [all rewards](/rewards/all-rewards/) and the [event recap](/guides/event-recap/).
