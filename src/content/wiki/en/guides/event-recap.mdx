The Hunt: Roblox 20 was Roblox's 20th-birthday event and the third Hunt. It ran **September 17–28, 2026**: twenty games, one per year from 2006 to 2025, each with a main quest and a secret quest. The join badge went out **29,902,983 times**.

## Timeline

- **Sept 9** — teased on Roblox socials with a cryptic “09 - 17 - 26”
- **Sept (Roblox Innovation Awards 2026)** — full announcement
- **Sept 14** — Roblox Plus early hub access
- **Sept 17, 3 PM PT** — public launch
- **Sept 27, 11 AM EST** — [final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/) unlocks
- **Sept 28, 10 AM PT** — event ends, all quests close

## How it worked

The hub had 20 islands, one per year. You visited them in order before free travel unlocked. Each island's [game](/games/) had a main quest (see [main prizes](/rewards/main-prizes/)) and a secret one (see [secret prizes](/rewards/secret-prizes/)). Clearing all 40 opened the final puzzle and its Amictus Infinitum. Around that: launcher quests (three Reignment items), Plus perks, an [11-item Robux shop](/rewards/marketplace-items/), and [join-year badges](/rewards/join-year-badges/) for your account's birth year.
