**Short answer: no for quest items, yes for leftovers already earned, and “it depends” for marketplace goods.** The Hunt: Roblox 20 ended on **September 28, 2026 at 10 AM PT**, and every quest in all 20 games closed with it.

## What's gone for good

- All 20 [main quest prizes](/rewards/main-prizes/) — the year items like Crossroads Tower and Duel of the Heights can no longer be earned.
- All 20 [secret quest prizes](/rewards/secret-prizes/).
- The **Amictus Infinitum** — the final puzzle closed with the event.
- The [join-year badges](/rewards/join-year-badges/) and launcher quest (Reignment) items.

## What you keep

Everything you earned stays in your inventory permanently — items, badges, and the Roblox Plus perks (Roblox Plus Hard Hat, Noob Assist: Birthday Bash, The Twentieth Teapot). Event quest items are **not tradeable**, so there is no legitimate way to buy an account-free copy.

## What might still be buyable

The 11 [marketplace items](/rewards/marketplace-items/) were sold for Robux during the event window; Roblox has not confirmed delisting dates for all of them, so some may remain purchasable for a short grace period. The one item with a genuine afterlife is the **Shedletsekkar** — sold as **limited unique**, it can resurface on the resale market at whatever price its owners demand.

## And the Dominus?

The **Dominus Infinitum** was never earnable by normal play — its badge still shows zero awards. If and when Roblox distributes it, the [winners page](/guides/winners/) will carry the news.

*This page reflects the state of the official badge API on September 29, 2026. If Roblox reopens any quest (past events have seen short grace windows), we will update.*
