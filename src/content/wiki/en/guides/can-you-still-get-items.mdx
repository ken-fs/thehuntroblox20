**Short answer: no.** The Hunt: Roblox 20 ended September 28, 2026 at 10 AM PT, and every quest closed with it.

## Gone for good

- All 20 [main prizes](/rewards/main-prizes/) and all 20 [secret prizes](/rewards/secret-prizes/)
- The **Amictus Infinitum** ([final puzzle](/guides/final-puzzle/))
- The [join-year badges](/rewards/join-year-badges/) and the three Reignment launcher items

## What you keep

Everything you earned stays in your inventory. Quest items are **not tradeable** — there's no legit way to buy them now.

## What might still be buyable

Some of the 11 [marketplace items](/rewards/marketplace-items/) may linger in the shop a while longer. The **Shedletsekkar** was sold as **limited unique**, so it can resurface on resale at whatever price owners set.

## The Dominus?

Never earnable in the first place — zero awards so far. Watch the [winners page](/guides/winners/).
