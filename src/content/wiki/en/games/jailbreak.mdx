**Jailbreak** was the **2017** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2017-2019: The Big Leagues era). Main quest reward: **A Bandit’s Bandana**. Secret quest reward: **Defective Despacito Spiders**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Jailbreak?

**Find and rescue five characters scattered across the map.** The item was made by UGC creator MiracleDrops.

**3,019,302 players** earned the badge (4.4% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Jailbreak?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Jailbreak's paid out the **Defective Despacito Spiders**. The steps were never written down properly — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “JB - Roblox 20 - Secret Quest.”

**410,999 players** earned it (3.6%) — versus 3,019,302 for the main quest.
