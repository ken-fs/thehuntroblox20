In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Jailbreak** represented the year **2017** in the **2017-2019: The Big Leagues** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **A Bandit’s Bandana** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Defective Despacito Spiders**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Jailbreak?

**Find and rescue five characters scattered across the map.** Clearing it awarded the **A Bandit’s Bandana**, a 2017-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator MiracleDrops. The reward badge was earned **3,019,302 times** (a 4.4% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Jailbreak?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Jailbreak's awarded the **Defective Despacito Spiders**. The community wiki still lists the exact steps as TBA — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “JB - Roblox 20 - Secret Quest.” What is fully confirmed: the reward itself and its rarity (below).

Only **410,999 players** earned the Defective Despacito Spiders badge (a 3.6% completion rate) — compare that to the 3,019,302 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Jailbreak?

Badimo's cops-and-robbers phenomenon dominated Roblox for years and set the standard for live-service updates on the platform. Its selection for 2017 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
