In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Roblox High School** represented the year **2016** in the **2014-2016: Digital Citizens** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Time Travel Schoolpack** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Team Create Workstation**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Roblox High School?

**Help Chuck complete his diploma.** Clearing it awarded the **Time Travel Schoolpack**, a 2016-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator simplyalemon. The reward badge was earned **2,543,596 times** (a 2.5% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Roblox High School?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Roblox High School's awarded the **Team Create Workstation**. Collect the required items and find the tool needed to reveal the hidden basement entrance and insert the items in the correct order to unlock the basement. Inside, collect all of the items in the room and enter a secret code to unlock the Green Door. Solve each room’s puzzle to obtain the keys and progress until the final area is unlocked, then collect the Scroll of Lore.

Only **325,116 players** earned the Team Create Workstation badge (a 2.1% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,543,596 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Roblox High School?

The definitive school roleplay game of its era, originally created by Cindering and carried on by its fan community. Its selection for 2016 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
