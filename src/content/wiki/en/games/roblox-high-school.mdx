**Roblox High School** was the **2016** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2014-2016: Digital Citizens era). Main quest reward: **Time Travel Schoolpack**. Secret quest reward: **Team Create Workstation**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Roblox High School?

**Help Chuck complete his diploma.** The item was made by UGC creator simplyalemon.

**2,543,596 players** earned the badge (2.5% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Roblox High School?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Roblox High School's paid out the **Team Create Workstation**. Collect the required items and find the tool needed to reveal the hidden basement entrance and insert the items in the correct order to unlock the basement. Inside, collect all of the items in the room and enter a secret code to unlock the Green Door. Solve each room’s puzzle to obtain the keys and progress until the final area is unlocked, then collect the Scroll of Lore.

**325,116 players** earned it (2.1%) — versus 2,543,596 for the main quest.
