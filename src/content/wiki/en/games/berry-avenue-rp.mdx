**Berry Avenue RP** was the **2022** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility era). Main quest reward: **The Berry Bindle**. Secret quest reward: **Sounds of the Past**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Berry Avenue RP?

**Complete a series of tasks in your classmates' dream job.** The item was made by UGC creator Lovespun.

**2,650,547 players** earned the badge (5.0% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Berry Avenue RP?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Berry Avenue RP's paid out the **Sounds of the Past**. Go to the secret Classic Tree and start the quest. Pick up an item from each store near the spawn, then return them to the tree. After being teleported, solve the puzzles and enter the portal once finished.

**341,038 players** earned it (2.7%) — versus 2,650,547 for the main quest.
