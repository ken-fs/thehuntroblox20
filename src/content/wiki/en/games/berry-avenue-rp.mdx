In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Berry Avenue RP** represented the year **2022** in the **2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **The Berry Bindle** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Sounds of the Past**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Berry Avenue RP?

**Complete a series of tasks in your classmates' dream job.** Clearing it awarded the **The Berry Bindle**, a 2022-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Lovespun. The reward badge was earned **2,650,547 times** (a 5.0% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Berry Avenue RP?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Berry Avenue RP's awarded the **Sounds of the Past**. Go to the secret Classic Tree and start the quest. Pick up an item from each store near the spawn, then return them to the tree. After being teleported, solve the puzzles and enter the portal once finished.

Only **341,038 players** earned the Sounds of the Past badge (a 2.7% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,650,547 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Berry Avenue RP?

Amberry Games' polished slice-of-life roleplay city became the genre's benchmark after Adopt Me. Its selection for 2022 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
