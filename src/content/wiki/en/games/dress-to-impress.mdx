**Dress To Impress** was the **2024** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2023-2025: New Classics era). Main quest reward: **The Runway Sunhat**. Secret quest reward: **Crown of Petals**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Dress To Impress?

**Play two rounds in the 2024 Dress To Impress place.** The item was made by UGC creator Dioncella.

**2,336,443 players** earned the badge (4.7% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Dress To Impress?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Dress To Impress's paid out the **Crown of Petals**. Talk to Lana and bring her seven different items, one at a time. After that, go through the portal to the original Dress To Impress and play two rounds.

**626,666 players** earned it (3.5%) — versus 2,336,443 for the main quest.
