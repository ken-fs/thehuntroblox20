In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Dress To Impress** represented the year **2024** in the **2023-2025: New Classics** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **The Runway Sunhat** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Crown of Petals**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Dress To Impress?

**Play two rounds in the 2024 Dress To Impress place.** Clearing it awarded the **The Runway Sunhat**, a 2024-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Dioncella. The reward badge was earned **2,336,443 times** (a 4.7% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Dress To Impress?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Dress To Impress's awarded the **Crown of Petals**. Talk to Lana and bring her seven different items, one at a time. After that, go through the portal to the original Dress To Impress and play two rounds.

Only **626,666 players** earned the Crown of Petals badge (a 3.5% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,336,443 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Dress To Impress?

The fashion runway game that took over Roblox (and TikTok) — theme, dress, walk, vote. Its selection for 2024 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
