In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Adopt Me!** represented the year **2019** in the **2017-2019: The Big Leagues** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **The KittyKånka** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Towering Pillar of UGC**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Adopt Me!?

**Hatch the Throwback Egg.** Clearing it awarded the **The KittyKånka**, a 2019-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator DawsonDelRey. The reward badge was earned **2,762,711 times** (a 3.9% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Adopt Me!?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Adopt Me!'s awarded the **Towering Pillar of UGC**. Find six hidden eggs and return them to Eggburt.

Only **510,768 players** earned the Towering Pillar of UGC badge (a 2.8% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,762,711 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Adopt Me!?

Uplift Games' pet roleplay giant is among the most-visited experiences in Roblox history and defined the pet-trading genre. Its selection for 2019 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
