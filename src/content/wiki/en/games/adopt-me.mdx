**Adopt Me!** was the **2019** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2017-2019: The Big Leagues era). Main quest reward: **The KittyKånka**. Secret quest reward: **Towering Pillar of UGC**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Adopt Me!?

**Hatch the Throwback Egg.** The item was made by UGC creator DawsonDelRey.

**2,762,711 players** earned the badge (3.9% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Adopt Me!?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Adopt Me!'s paid out the **Towering Pillar of UGC**. Find six hidden eggs and return them to Eggburt.

**510,768 players** earned it (2.8%) — versus 2,762,711 for the main quest.
