In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Chaos Canyon** represented the year **2009** in the **2008-2010: The Early Grind** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Crest of the Canyon** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Three Clubs**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Chaos Canyon?

**Get four KOs in the Crest of the Canyon.** Clearing it awarded the **Crest of the Canyon**, a 2009-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Jazzyx3. The reward badge was earned **5,535,672 times** (a 2.9% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Chaos Canyon?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Chaos Canyon's awarded the **The Three Clubs**. Collect the Rare Armor and start five challenging tasks by entering each code on the wall one at a time. Complete each task.

Only **250,736 players** earned the The Three Clubs badge (a 2.0% completion rate) — compare that to the 5,535,672 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Chaos Canyon?

A Roblox-built brickbattle map stacked across a multi-level canyon, home to some of the platform’s earliest clan wars. Its selection for 2009 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
