**Chaos Canyon** was the **2009** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2008-2010: The Early Grind era). Main quest reward: **Crest of the Canyon**. Secret quest reward: **The Three Clubs**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Chaos Canyon?

**Get four KOs in the Crest of the Canyon.** The item was made by UGC creator Jazzyx3.

**5,535,672 players** earned the badge (2.9% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Chaos Canyon?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Chaos Canyon's paid out the **The Three Clubs**. Collect the Rare Armor and start five challenging tasks by entering each code on the wall one at a time. Complete each task.

**250,736 players** earned it (2.0%) — versus 5,535,672 for the main quest.
