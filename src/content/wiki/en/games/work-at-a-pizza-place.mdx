**Work at a Pizza Place** was the **2010** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2008-2010: The Early Grind era). Main quest reward: **Builder Brothers’ Balancing Act**. Secret quest reward: **Gear Hoarder Loadout Belt**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Work at a Pizza Place?

**Travel back in time with a mysterious figure and steal the secret recipe.** The item was made by UGC creator blushannie.

**4,515,982 players** earned the badge (3.3% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Work at a Pizza Place?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Work at a Pizza Place's paid out the **Gear Hoarder Loadout Belt**. Enter a secret code in the time machine and return the secret recipe to Tony. After that, complete a series of difficult tasks and send c00lkid back to his era.

**465,797 players** earned it (1.9%) — versus 4,515,982 for the main quest.
