In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Work at a Pizza Place** represented the year **2010** in the **2008-2010: The Early Grind** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Builder Brothers’ Balancing Act** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Gear Hoarder Loadout Belt**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Work at a Pizza Place?

**Travel back in time with a mysterious figure and steal the secret recipe.** Clearing it awarded the **Builder Brothers’ Balancing Act**, a 2010-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator blushannie. The reward badge was earned **4,515,982 times** (a 3.3% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Work at a Pizza Place?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Work at a Pizza Place's awarded the **Gear Hoarder Loadout Belt**. Enter a secret code in the time machine and return the secret recipe to Tony. After that, complete a series of difficult tasks and send c00lkid back to his era.

Only **465,797 players** earned the Gear Hoarder Loadout Belt badge (a 1.9% completion rate) — compare that to the 4,515,982 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Work at a Pizza Place?

Dued1's job simulator is one of the most-visited Roblox games of all time and the blueprint for the entire roleplay-job genre. Its selection for 2010 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
