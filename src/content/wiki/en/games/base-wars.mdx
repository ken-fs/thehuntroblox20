**Base Wars!** was the **2012** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2011-2013: Against All Odds era). Main quest reward: **Manliest Man Award**. Secret quest reward: **Quad Sword Aura**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Base Wars!?

**Win a round or play two rounds of Skirmish.** The item was made by UGC creator inkwaves.

**3,170,838 players** earned the badge (2.3% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Base Wars!?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Base Wars!'s paid out the **Quad Sword Aura**. Select PvP mode and use a loadout consisting of Classic equipment. Go to the hidden cave and touch the statue to be teleported to The Trials mode. Complete a match of The Trials with three other players.

**219,791 players** earned it (2.2%) — versus 3,170,838 for the main quest.
