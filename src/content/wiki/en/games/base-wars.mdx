In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Base Wars!** represented the year **2012** in the **2011-2013: Against All Odds** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Manliest Man Award** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Quad Sword Aura**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Base Wars!?

**Win a round or play two rounds of Skirmish.** Clearing it awarded the **Manliest Man Award**, a 2012-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator inkwaves. The reward badge was earned **3,170,838 times** (a 2.3% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Base Wars!?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Base Wars!'s awarded the **Quad Sword Aura**. Select PvP mode and use a loadout consisting of Classic equipment. Go to the hidden cave and touch the statue to be teleported to The Trials mode. Complete a match of The Trials with three other players.

Only **219,791 players** earned the Quad Sword Aura badge (a 2.2% completion rate) — compare that to the 3,170,838 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Base Wars!?

A vehicle-and-infantry base combat classic from the era when lobby shooters ruled the front page. Its selection for 2012 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
