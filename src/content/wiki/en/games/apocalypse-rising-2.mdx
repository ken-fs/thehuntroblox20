**Apocalypse Rising 2** was the **2013** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2011-2013: Against All Odds era). Main quest reward: **Biting BLOXikin**. Secret quest reward: **The Magic of Creation**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Apocalypse Rising 2?

**Complete a set of quests.** The item was made by UGC creator Innovatement.

**2,402,559 players** earned the badge (2.1% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Apocalypse Rising 2?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Apocalypse Rising 2's paid out the **The Magic of Creation**. Locate the seven pages of Smiff's Journal scattered across the map and read them in order.

**258,453 players** earned it (1.8%) — versus 2,402,559 for the main quest.
