In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Apocalypse Rising 2** represented the year **2013** in the **2011-2013: Against All Odds** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Biting BLOXikin** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Magic of Creation**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Apocalypse Rising 2?

**Complete a set of quests.** Clearing it awarded the **Biting BLOXikin**, a 2013-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Innovatement. The reward badge was earned **2,402,559 times** (a 2.1% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Apocalypse Rising 2?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Apocalypse Rising 2's awarded the **The Magic of Creation**. Locate the seven pages of Smiff's Journal scattered across the map and read them in order.

Only **258,453 players** earned the The Magic of Creation badge (a 1.8% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,402,559 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Apocalypse Rising 2?

Dualpoint's open-world zombie survival sequel stands in for the original Apocalypse Rising, the game that made 2013 the year of Roblox survival. Its selection for 2013 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
