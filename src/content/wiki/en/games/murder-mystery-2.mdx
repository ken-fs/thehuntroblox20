In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Murder Mystery 2** represented the year **2014** in the **2014-2016: Digital Citizens** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Knife-Dodger's Bighead** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Year of the Bloxxer**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Murder Mystery 2?

**Collect 50 coins during the rounds.** Clearing it awarded the **Knife-Dodger's Bighead**, a 2014-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Clockset. The reward badge was earned **4,536,059 times** (a 6.4% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Murder Mystery 2?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Murder Mystery 2's awarded the **The Year of the Bloxxer**. Find the three items across the three 2014 maps.

Only **499,114 players** earned the The Year of the Bloxxer badge (a 4.4% completion rate) — compare that to the 4,536,059 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Murder Mystery 2?

Nikilis's social-deduction phenomenon — murderer, sheriff, innocents — became one of the most-played games in Roblox history. Its selection for 2014 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
