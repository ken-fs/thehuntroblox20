**Murder Mystery 2** was the **2014** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2014-2016: Digital Citizens era). Main quest reward: **Knife-Dodger's Bighead**. Secret quest reward: **The Year of the Bloxxer**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Murder Mystery 2?

**Collect 50 coins during the rounds.** The item was made by UGC creator Clockset.

**4,536,059 players** earned the badge (6.4% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Murder Mystery 2?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Murder Mystery 2's paid out the **The Year of the Bloxxer**. Find the three items across the three 2014 maps.

**499,114 players** earned it (4.4%) — versus 4,536,059 for the main quest.
