**Build A Boat For Treasure** was the **2018** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2017-2019: The Big Leagues era). Main quest reward: **Build A Boat for a Top Hat**. Secret quest reward: **The Archivist's Handbook**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Build A Boat For Treasure?

**Reach the bakery area and bake an anniversary cake.** The item was made by UGC creator fizzysaur.

**3,003,450 players** earned the badge (3.7% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Build A Boat For Treasure?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Build A Boat For Treasure's paid out the **The Archivist's Handbook**. Open the build menu and type "a piece of the past" in the search bar. Collect all 25 puzzle pieces, then put them together to complete the puzzle.

**446,254 players** earned it (2.9%) — versus 3,003,450 for the main quest.
