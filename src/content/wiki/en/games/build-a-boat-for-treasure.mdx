In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Build A Boat For Treasure** represented the year **2018** in the **2017-2019: The Big Leagues** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Build A Boat for a Top Hat** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Archivist's Handbook**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Build A Boat For Treasure?

**Reach the bakery area and bake an anniversary cake.** Clearing it awarded the **Build A Boat for a Top Hat**, a 2018-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator fizzysaur. The reward badge was earned **3,003,450 times** (a 3.7% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Build A Boat For Treasure?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Build A Boat For Treasure's awarded the **The Archivist's Handbook**. Open the build menu and type "a piece of the past" in the search bar. Collect all 25 puzzle pieces, then put them together to complete the puzzle.

Only **446,254 players** earned the The Archivist's Handbook badge (a 2.9% completion rate) — compare that to the 3,003,450 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Build A Boat For Treasure?

Chillz Studios' build-and-sail sandbox turned janky physics boats into one of the platform's most enduring hits. Its selection for 2018 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
