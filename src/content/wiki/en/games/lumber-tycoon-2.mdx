**Lumber Tycoon 2** was the **2015** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2014-2016: Digital Citizens era). Main quest reward: **Leather Adventure Backpack**. Secret quest reward: **Chest of Overflowing Loot**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Lumber Tycoon 2?

**Fill Geck's deposit with wood until it's full.** The item was made by UGC creator wuvenote.

**3,038,616 players** earned the badge (3.2% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Lumber Tycoon 2?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Lumber Tycoon 2's paid out the **Chest of Overflowing Loot**. The steps were never written down properly — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “LT2 - You got glorbed!.”

**234,130 players** earned it (2.7%) — versus 3,038,616 for the main quest.
