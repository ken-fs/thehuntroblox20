In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Lumber Tycoon 2** represented the year **2015** in the **2014-2016: Digital Citizens** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Leather Adventure Backpack** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Chest of Overflowing Loot**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Lumber Tycoon 2?

**Fill Geck's deposit with wood until it's full.** Clearing it awarded the **Leather Adventure Backpack**, a 2015-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator wuvenote. The reward badge was earned **3,038,616 times** (a 3.2% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Lumber Tycoon 2?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Lumber Tycoon 2's awarded the **Chest of Overflowing Loot**. The community wiki still lists the exact steps as TBA — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “LT2 - You got glorbed!.” What is fully confirmed: the reward itself and its rarity (below).

Only **234,130 players** earned the Chest of Overflowing Loot badge (a 2.7% completion rate) — compare that to the 3,038,616 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Lumber Tycoon 2?

Defaultio's open-ended tycoon sandbox turned chopping wood into one of the most beloved economy games on the platform. Its selection for 2015 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
