**Blade Ball** was the **2023** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2023-2025: New Classics era). Main quest reward: **The Last Deflection**. Secret quest reward: **Hands-On Experience**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Blade Ball?

**Collect three tickets during the rounds.** The item was made by UGC creator Innovatement.

**1,956,388 players** earned the badge (3.0% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Blade Ball?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Blade Ball's paid out the **Hands-On Experience**. Kill the NPC while having equipped the Serpentbane Sword and queue up after 2 others or more have also killed the NPC to defeat a boss.

**322,054 players** earned it (1.6%) — versus 1,956,388 for the main quest.
