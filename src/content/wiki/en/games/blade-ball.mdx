In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Blade Ball** represented the year **2023** in the **2023-2025: New Classics** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **The Last Deflection** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Hands-On Experience**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Blade Ball?

**Collect three tickets during the rounds.** Clearing it awarded the **The Last Deflection**, a 2023-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Innovatement. The reward badge was earned **1,956,388 times** (a 3.0% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Blade Ball?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Blade Ball's awarded the **Hands-On Experience**. Kill the NPC while having equipped the Serpentbane Sword and queue up after 2 others or more have also killed the NPC to defeat a boss.

Only **322,054 players** earned the Hands-On Experience badge (a 1.6% completion rate) — compare that to the 1,956,388 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Blade Ball?

Wiggity's deflect-the-ball duel game went from zero to front-page phenomenon on pure timing-and-mindgames gameplay. Its selection for 2023 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
