**Rocket Arena** was the **2008** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2008-2010: The Early Grind era). Main quest reward: **Brick-Blast Jetpack**. Secret quest reward: **The Eggsplorer’s Paintball Mask**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Rocket Arena?

**Get five KOs the in Rocket Arena.** The item was made by UGC creator FileSaved.

**5,743,821 players** earned the badge (3.0% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Rocket Arena?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Rocket Arena's paid out the **The Eggsplorer’s Paintball Mask**. Enter the phone number shown on a billboard using the SpawnLocation as a keypad, then observe the billboard and start the race. Follow the checkpoints in the correct order. After receiving the “Chimney, huh?” message, head to the start of the obby, jump into the pole, and touch the flag.

**299,254 players** earned it (1.8%) — versus 5,743,821 for the main quest.
