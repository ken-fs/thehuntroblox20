In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Rocket Arena** represented the year **2008** in the **2008-2010: The Early Grind** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Brick-Blast Jetpack** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Eggsplorer’s Paintball Mask**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Rocket Arena?

**Get five KOs the in Rocket Arena.** Clearing it awarded the **Brick-Blast Jetpack**, a 2008-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator FileSaved. The reward badge was earned **5,743,821 times** (a 3.0% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Rocket Arena?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Rocket Arena's awarded the **The Eggsplorer’s Paintball Mask**. Enter the phone number shown on a billboard using the SpawnLocation as a keypad, then observe the billboard and start the race. Follow the checkpoints in the correct order. After receiving the “Chimney, huh?” message, head to the start of the obby, jump into the pole, and touch the flag.

Only **299,254 players** earned the The Eggsplorer’s Paintball Mask badge (a 1.8% completion rate) — compare that to the 5,743,821 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Rocket Arena?

The classic Rockets-only brickbattle — destructible bridges, no guns, and some of the earliest large-scale chaos on the platform. Its selection for 2008 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
