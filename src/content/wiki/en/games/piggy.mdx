**Piggy** was the **2021** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility era). Main quest reward: **Willow’s Legacy Hood**. Secret quest reward: **Sparkwave Headset**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Piggy?

**Complete any two chapters in the book 1.** The item was made by UGC creator s2ouls.

**3,028,968 players** earned the badge (6.1% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Piggy?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Piggy's paid out the **Sparkwave Headset**. Enter House - Chapter 1 map and use the wrench to reveal a hidden shovel in the basement. Use the shovel to dig a underground hole behind the well in the garden and escape.

**729,837 players** earned it (3.0%) — versus 3,028,968 for the main quest.
