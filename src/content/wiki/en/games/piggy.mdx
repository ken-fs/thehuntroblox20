In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Piggy** represented the year **2021** in the **2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Willow’s Legacy Hood** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Sparkwave Headset**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Piggy?

**Complete any two chapters in the book 1.** Clearing it awarded the **Willow’s Legacy Hood**, a 2021-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator s2ouls. The reward badge was earned **3,028,968 times** (a 6.1% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Piggy?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Piggy's awarded the **Sparkwave Headset**. Enter House - Chapter 1 map and use the wrench to reveal a hidden shovel in the basement. Use the shovel to dig a underground hole behind the well in the garden and escape.

Only **729,837 players** earned the Sparkwave Headset badge (a 3.0% completion rate) — compare that to the 3,028,968 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Piggy?

The episodic horror-escape phenomenon that had the whole platform theorizing about its story between chapters. Its selection for 2021 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
