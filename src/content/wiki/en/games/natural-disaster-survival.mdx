**Natural Disaster Survival** was the **2011** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2011-2013: Against All Odds era). Main quest reward: **The Disaster Forecaster**. Secret quest reward: **Personal Safety Spikes**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Natural Disaster Survival?

**Rescue three animals.** The item was made by UGC creator Innovatement.

**4,243,846 players** earned the badge (3.6% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Natural Disaster Survival?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Natural Disaster Survival's paid out the **Personal Safety Spikes**. Go to the hidden control room in the lobby's center and change canCollectHats to true. Collect hats dropped from dead players and donate it to the box in the control room.

**709,190 players** earned it (2.4%) — versus 4,243,846 for the main quest.
