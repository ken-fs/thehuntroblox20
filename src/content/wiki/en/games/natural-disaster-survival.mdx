In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Natural Disaster Survival** represented the year **2011** in the **2011-2013: Against All Odds** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **The Disaster Forecaster** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Personal Safety Spikes**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Natural Disaster Survival?

**Rescue three animals.** Clearing it awarded the **The Disaster Forecaster**, a 2011-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Innovatement. The reward badge was earned **4,243,846 times** (a 3.6% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Natural Disaster Survival?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Natural Disaster Survival's awarded the **Personal Safety Spikes**. Go to the hidden control room in the lobby's center and change canCollectHats to true. Collect hats dropped from dead players and donate it to the box in the control room.

Only **709,190 players** earned the Personal Safety Spikes badge (a 2.4% completion rate) — compare that to the 4,243,846 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Natural Disaster Survival?

Stickmasterluke's round-based survival classic — one map, one disaster, 60 seconds to live — has been a platform staple for over a decade. Its selection for 2011 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
