In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Sword Fights on the Heights IV** represented the year **2007** in the **2006-2007: Where It All Began** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Duel of the Heights** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **How To Build A Happy Home**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Sword Fights on the Heights IV?

**Collect any four swords in the Sword Fight on the Heights.** Clearing it awarded the **Duel of the Heights**, a 2007-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Spectaqual. The reward badge was earned **6,300,158 times** (a 4.1% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Sword Fights on the Heights IV?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Sword Fights on the Heights IV's awarded the **How To Build A Happy Home**. The community wiki still lists the exact steps as TBA — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “The Signature Shedletsky Secret.” What is fully confirmed: the reward itself and its rarity (below).

Only **196,968 players** earned the How To Build A Happy Home badge (a 2.3% completion rate) — compare that to the 6,300,158 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Sword Fights on the Heights IV?

Shedletsky's arena swordfighter is one of the most influential PvP games in Roblox history and the template for a decade of sword games. Its selection for 2007 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
