**Sword Fights on the Heights IV** was the **2007** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2006-2007: Where It All Began era). Main quest reward: **Duel of the Heights**. Secret quest reward: **How To Build A Happy Home**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Sword Fights on the Heights IV?

**Collect any four swords in the Sword Fight on the Heights.** The item was made by UGC creator Spectaqual.

**6,300,158 players** earned the badge (4.1% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Sword Fights on the Heights IV?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Sword Fights on the Heights IV's paid out the **How To Build A Happy Home**. The steps were never written down properly — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “The Signature Shedletsky Secret.”

**196,968 players** earned it (2.3%) — versus 6,300,158 for the main quest.
