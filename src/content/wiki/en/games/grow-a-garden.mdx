**Grow a Garden** was the **2025** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2023-2025: New Classics era). Main quest reward: **Grown From a Garden**. Secret quest reward: **The Huntmaster's Visor**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Grow a Garden?

**Steal 50 fruits from Wise Owl.** The item was made by UGC creator ReddieTheTeddy.

**2,385,559 players** earned the badge (4.8% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Grow a Garden?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Grow a Garden's paid out the **The Huntmaster's Visor**. The steps were never written down properly — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “GaG - Down Under.”

**556,491 players** earned it (4.2%) — versus 2,385,559 for the main quest.
