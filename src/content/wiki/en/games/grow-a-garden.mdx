In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Grow a Garden** represented the year **2025** in the **2023-2025: New Classics** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Grown From a Garden** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **The Huntmaster's Visor**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Grow a Garden?

**Steal 50 fruits from Wise Owl.** Clearing it awarded the **Grown From a Garden**, a 2025-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator ReddieTheTeddy. The reward badge was earned **2,385,559 times** (a 4.8% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in Grow a Garden?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Grow a Garden's awarded the **The Huntmaster's Visor**. The community wiki still lists the exact steps as TBA — the only official trace is the badge art, captioned “GaG - Down Under.” What is fully confirmed: the reward itself and its rarity (below).

Only **556,491 players** earned the The Huntmaster's Visor badge (a 4.2% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,385,559 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.
