In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **World // Zero** represented the year **2020** in the **2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Hounds of the Zero** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Neo Virtual Headliner**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in World // Zero?

**Finish the OG Tower.** Clearing it awarded the **Hounds of the Zero**, a 2020-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator DawsonDelRey. The reward badge was earned **2,374,633 times** (a 3.3% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it a commonly earned reward.

## What was the secret quest in World // Zero?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — World // Zero's awarded the **Neo Virtual Headliner**. Go to the hidden area in the well and start the quest. Collect four Relics scattered across map and return to go through the portal to start a level. Progress through the level and defeat the final boss.

Only **263,459 players** earned the Neo Virtual Headliner badge (a 2.2% completion rate) — compare that to the 2,374,633 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why World // Zero?

A full anime-style MMO RPG with classes, dungeons and raids — proof of how far Roblox engines could be pushed by 2020. Its selection for 2020 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
