**World // Zero** was the **2020** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2020-2022: Worlds of Possibility era). Main quest reward: **Hounds of the Zero**. Secret quest reward: **Neo Virtual Headliner**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in World // Zero?

**Finish the OG Tower.** The item was made by UGC creator DawsonDelRey.

**2,374,633 players** earned the badge (3.3% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in World // Zero?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. World // Zero's paid out the **Neo Virtual Headliner**. Go to the hidden area in the well and start the quest. Collect four Relics scattered across map and return to go through the portal to start a level. Progress through the level and defeat the final boss.

**263,459 players** earned it (2.2%) — versus 2,374,633 for the main quest.
