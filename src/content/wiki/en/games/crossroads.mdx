**Crossroads** was the **2006** pick in The Hunt: Roblox 20 (2006-2007: Where It All Began era). Main quest reward: **Crossroads Tower**. Secret quest reward: **Original Web Elements**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026; both quests are closed.

## What was the main quest in Crossroads?

**Get three KOs in the Crossroads.** The item was made by UGC creator Reverse_Polarity.

**7,214,915 players** earned the badge (3.8% of those who tried).

## What was the secret quest in Crossroads?

Every game in the event hid a second quest off the beaten path. Crossroads's paid out the **Original Web Elements**. Interact with the Quality Homes billboard in the Thieves' Den to make the map repeat endlessly, and collect 4 pink gifts.

**872,662 players** earned it (2.8%) — versus 7,214,915 for the main quest.
