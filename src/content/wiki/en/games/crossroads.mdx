In The Hunt: Roblox 20, **Crossroads** represented the year **2006** in the **2006-2007: Where It All Began** era. Clearing its main quest awarded the **Crossroads Tower** avatar item, and a hidden secret quest inside the same game awarded the **Original Web Elements**. The event ran September 17–28, 2026 — both quests are now closed, but the earned items remain in players' inventories.

## What was the main quest in Crossroads?

**Get three KOs in the Crossroads.** Clearing it awarded the **Crossroads Tower**, a 2006-themed avatar item designed by UGC creator Reverse_Polarity. The reward badge was earned **7,214,915 times** (a 3.8% completion rate among players who attempted it), making it one of the most-earned rewards of the event.

## What was the secret quest in Crossroads?

Every one of the 20 participating games hid a second, undocumented quest — Crossroads's awarded the **Original Web Elements**. Interact with the Quality Homes billboard in the Thieves' Den to make the map repeat endlessly, and collect 4 pink gifts.

Only **872,662 players** earned the Original Web Elements badge (a 2.8% completion rate) — compare that to the 7,214,915 who finished the main quest. Secret rewards were the real completionist chase of The Hunt: Roblox 20.

## Why Crossroads?

One of the original Roblox brickbattles, built by the Roblox team itself — the rocket-and-sword arena that defined classic Roblox PvP. Its selection for 2006 made it one of just 20 games — one per year of Roblox history — featured in the anniversary event.
